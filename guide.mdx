title: "How to use Polywatcher"
description: "A quick tour of Live Trades, Whale orders, Liquidations, Leaderboard and wallet pages."
---

Go to [polywatcher.com](https://polywatcher.com) and pick a page from the menu. No account is needed for Free.

## Live Trades

Orders and liquidations of \$1K or more on Polymarket Perps, newest first. Each row shows the asset, the side and the value; Plus adds the wallet behind it. Filter by asset class (Crypto, Stocks, Commodities, Indexes) or by a single asset such as
BTC or NVDA. Tap a row for its details. On Free, the orders are a random selection, the same for everyone; every
liquidation is listed.

## How to read a row

- OPEN (blue) means the order opened a position, or more of one. CLOSE (magenta) means it closed part or all of one.
- LONG gains when the price rises. SHORT gains when it falls.
- On Plus, a wallet that is not known yet reads "Identifying" until its name arrives.

## Whale orders

Orders of \$10K or more. On Free this is a selection of them, the same for everyone.

## Liquidations

Each liquidation of \$1K or more, with its realized P&L (its wallet on Plus).

## Leaderboard

The top 25 wallets, recorded every hour. On Free, each rank shows its figures; Plus names the wallet and opens its page.

## Wallet pages

On Plus, a wallet's P&L, its latest orders and liquidations, and its ranks. Use the search box to find a wallet by name or
address. Every wallet and every event has its own link, so you can share what you find.

## Telegram

Our free Telegram channel posts a selection of big moves.
