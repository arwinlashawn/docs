## Free

Free costs \$0, with no card and no sign-up. It shows:

- Orders and liquidations of \$1K or more, with the wallet behind them once identified, 15 minutes behind.
- The latest 100 on Live Trades, the latest 50 liquidations, and the latest 50 whale orders (a selection, the same
  for everyone).
- The last 7 days.
- The latest 25 orders and liquidations on a wallet's page.
- The top 25 of each leaderboard, recorded every hour.
- The trading style of 3 wallets a month.
- Our Telegram channel.

## Plus

Everything in Free, plus:

- 5 minutes behind.
- Every order, whale order and liquidation we record, with no limit on the lists.
- Each order whole, with its fills combined into one row at the average price.
- Each order's type, market or limit, with a filter for it.
- Each wallet's trading style and type.
- 30 days of history.
- Personal alerts on 10 wallets or assets, sent to your own Telegram.

## Pro

Everything in Plus, plus:

- Real time.
- The leverage behind each order and open position, when we can read it.
- The full history of everything we record.
- Personal alerts on 100 wallets or assets.
- Early access to new features.

Planned for Pro: each wallet's track record (win rate and P&L per trade), net long and short positioning, and data
export.

## Prices

- Plus: \$14.99 a month, or \$9.99 a month billed yearly (\$119.88 a year).
- Plus on the waitlist: \$4.99 a month for your first year, billed yearly (\$59.88).
- Pro: priced at launch.

Prices are in US dollars and not final. They depend on how fast the waitlist grows and which features people want most,
and they may come down as demand grows.
