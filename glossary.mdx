---
title: "Glossary"
description: "The words Polywatcher uses."
---

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Long** / **Short** | The side of a position. A long gains when the price rises. |
| **OPEN** / **CLOSE** | What an order did. OPEN opened a position, or more of one. CLOSE closed part or all of one. |
| **Buy** / **Sell** | An order's direction, shown only when the position it changed is not known yet. |
| **Value** | An order's dollar size. |
| **Size** | A quantity of the asset itself (for example 2.5 ETH). |
| **Whale order** | An order of $10K or more. |
| **Liquidated** | A position closed by force because the account fell below its margin. |
| **P&L** | Profit and loss. On the leaderboard, after fees. |
| **Account value** | What an account holds, including open positions at their current price. |
| **Leverage** | Position value divided by the margin it uses. |
| **Identifying** | The wallet behind an order is not known yet. |
| **Stocks, Crypto, Commodities, Indexes** | The four market categories. |
