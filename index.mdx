---

Polywatcher is a whale tracker for Polymarket Perps, Polymarket's perpetual futures. It shows large orders and liquidations,
with the wallet behind each one once identified, hourly top 25 leaderboards, and a page for every wallet.

New here? Start with [how to use Polywatcher](/guide), then see [what Free, Plus and Pro include](/free-and-pro).

- Whale orders: orders of \$10K or more.
- Liquidations: positions closed by force when losses use up the margin.
- Wallets: P&L, orders, liquidations and ranks for each wallet.
- Leaderboards: the top 25, recorded every hour.

Questions? Write to [support@polywatcher.com](mailto:support@polywatcher.com).

Polywatcher shows what wallets did, never what anyone should do. Nothing here is advice.
